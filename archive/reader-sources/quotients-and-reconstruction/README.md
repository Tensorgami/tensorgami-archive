This archive contains the complete 16 native TeX inputs for the published
82-page PDF and matching full web exposition. Compile main.tex with pdfLaTeX
until references and the table of contents stabilize (normally three runs on
a clean build). The preamble lists the required standard packages, including
TikZ-CD. Fonts and TeX versions may affect pagination or PDF bytes; the
website supplies the published PDF.

Mathematical text: clarification edition of 22 October 2026. Three explanatory
passages name the scaled shear, distinguish representative choice from the
canonical inclusion, and state the ambient assumptions for higher quotient
and descent results.
